## The problem

Cerebral palsy is the most common physical disability in childhood. Children with it often need individual learning support, which can leave them feeling isolated from their classmates. The exercises that build their muscle control and stability happen outside the school curriculum, and outside the games everyone else plays.

<Metrics items={[{ value: "Top 3", label: "James Dyson Award, Ireland 2018" }, { value: "DRS 2018", label: "Exhibited at the Design Research Society conference" }]} />

## The idea

Mylo is an interactive ultra-short-throw projector for the classroom. It turns a desk, the floor or a wall into a touch surface with games and activities that build fine and gross motor skills. Any child can join in, whatever their physical ability.

<Callout label="Design decision">
Design for the whole class, not the diagnosis. The games are fun for every child, so the child who needs the exercise isn't singled out while doing it.
</Callout>
