An undergraduate case-bridging and redesign project: take a category of product and rethink it from first principles. The DEV X is a concept for Sony and is not affiliated with the brand.

## Less, but better

Recording binoculars tend to pile on controls. The DEV X follows Dieter Rams' "form follows function": do the job at the highest level with the least complexity.

The injection-moulded body is stripped back to its purest form, which lowers the amount of material and the manufacturing cost.

## Two details

  src="/work/dev-x/details.jpg"

<Callout label="Design decision">
One control, two jobs. The eye-piece adjuster on the rear is also the power button. Press it and the binoculars come to life, which removes a button without removing a function.
</Callout>

The whole interface is five buttons. Mode, Stop, Camera and Playback sit around a central D-pad, with a discreet record button at its centre.
