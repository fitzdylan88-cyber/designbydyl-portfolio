An undergraduate industrial design project: pick a product category and rethink it from scratch. The DEV X is a concept for Sony and is not affiliated with the brand.

## Less, but better

Recording binoculars tend to pile on controls. I took Dieter Rams' "less, but better" as the brief: do the job well, with as little complexity as possible.

<Figure

I stripped the injection-moulded body back as far as it would go, which cuts the material used and the cost to make it.

## Two details

<Figure
  src="/work/dev-x/details.jpg"
  width={2400}

  wide

<Callout label="Design decision">
One control, two jobs. The eye-piece adjuster on the rear is also the power button. Press it and the binoculars come to life, which removes a button without removing a function.
</Callout>

The whole interface is five buttons. Mode, Stop, Camera and Playback sit around a central D-pad, with a discreet record button at its centre.
