Digital rendering is a quick way to visualise a product or concept. This series, painted entirely on the iPad Pro, was practice in building form, material and light from layers and textures. The subjects were things I love looking at: trainers from Adidas, Balenciaga and Nike, plus cars and a Nintendo Switch.
