A short design challenge: rebrand the de Young Museum in Golden Gate Park. I combined the sinuous, organic lines of Art Nouveau with the museum's modernist architecture. The building's silhouette becomes a mask filled with floral pattern, and the mark works in several colourways across stationery, signage and the web. This is an unsolicited concept and not affiliated with the museum.
