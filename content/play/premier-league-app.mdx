A self-initiated redesign of the Premier League app. The landing feed pairs news with each club's live updates in a stories row at the top. Around it: articles you can save for offline reading, highlights and behind-the-scenes video, fixtures with broadcast schedules, and player profiles that track a season's progress. This is an unofficial concept and not affiliated with the Premier League.

<Figure src="/work/premier-league-app/fixtures.jpg" width={1920} height={1080} alt="The fixtures screen showing upcoming matches and broadcast schedules" wide />

I started on paper, sketching each screen and the flow between them, then built the screens up digitally from those wireframes.
