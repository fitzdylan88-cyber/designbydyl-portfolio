Runcoin is a fun, responsive exercise app for tracking runs and walks. The mountain backdrop gives it an outdoor feel, and it can be themed to your own colours. The dashboard keeps things simple: steps front and centre, with calories burned and Runcoins earned on either side. It's designed for phone and tablet.
