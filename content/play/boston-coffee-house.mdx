A brand for a small standing espresso bar between Chinatown, South Station and the Financial District. It's a place that puts hospitality on a par with the coffee. The identity pairs the friendly Pacifico script with topographic line work and a colour gradient. It rotates through four seasonal colourways, so signage, cups and bags change with the year.
