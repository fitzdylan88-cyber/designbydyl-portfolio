## The idea

Cafe reviews score the whole place, but that's rarely why you go back. You go back for the flat white, or the cinnamon bun. CUPS lets you rate **those**.

## What it does

- **Discover** nearby cafes on a map.
- **Rate individual items**, such as a coffee or a pastry, with a photo.
- **Collect stamps** in a personal cafe passport as you visit new places.
- **Compare notes** through community ratings and leaderboards.

<Callout label="Design decision">
Make rating feel like collecting. Stamps in a passport and a spot on the leaderboard give you a reason to log the next coffee too.
</Callout>

## Built as a PWA

It installs to your home screen straight from the browser, with no app store.

CUPS isn't public. It's a personal project that started as an idea I wanted to try out.
