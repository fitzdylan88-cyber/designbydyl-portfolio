## What it does

Raw Pokémon cards are priced from market data, and graded and sports cards from real **sold** listings rather than asking prices. A daily job keeps a rolling record of sales, so every card is valued at a median instead of one outlier.

<Callout label="Design decision">
Sold prices, not asking prices. What people actually paid is the only number worth trusting.
</Callout>
